# Where do NIH's institutes fund data science abroad?

**Laura Ellis** · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laura-ellis-profile/) · FY2016 to FY2026

For the 10 institutes in the [opportunity analysis](../ic_gaps/README.md), I traced data science funding outside the United States, both direct awards to foreign organizations and subawards from U.S. grantees to foreign partners. Each page shows one chart and the headline findings. The full reports are internal.

| Institute | Name | Data science funding abroad through U.S. partners |
|---|---|---:|
| [NCI](nci.md) | National Cancer Institute | 55% |
| [NHLBI](nhlbi.md) | National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute | 73% |
| [NIA](nia.md) | National Institute on Aging | 71% |
| [NIAID](niaid.md) | National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases | 66% |
| [NICHD](nichd.md) | Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development | 79% |
| [NIDA](nida.md) | National Institute on Drug Abuse | 79% |
| [NIDDK](niddk.md) | National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases | 93% |
| [NIGMS](nigms.md) | National Institute of General Medical Sciences | 89% |
| [NIMH](nimh.md) | National Institute of Mental Health | 69% |
| [NINDS](ninds.md) | National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke | 80% |

Code: `direct_awards.py` and `subawards.py` (NIH-wide tables), `regions.py` (country lookup), `ic_global.py` (institute tables), `teasers.py` (these pages), `map_data.py` (data for the interactive map on the [site](https://laura-ellis.github.io/projects/nih-ds-global.html)).
