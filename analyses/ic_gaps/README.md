# Where is data science thin at NIH's 10 largest institutes?

**Laura Ellis** · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laura-ellis-profile/) · FY2016 to FY2025

For the 10 institutes with the most data science projects, I looked for the places where data science is thin: against the rest of NIH, against each institute's own research areas, and against disease burden. Each page shows one chart and the headline findings. The full reports are internal.

| Institute | Name | Data science share of funding |
|---|---|---:|
| [NCI](nci.md) | National Cancer Institute | 26% |

Code: `ic_gaps.py` (gaps), `nofo_asks.py` and `nofo_panel.py` (NOFOs and projects), `ic_outputs.py` (outputs and levers), `teasers.py` (these pages).
