# Where can data science grow at NIH's 10 largest institutes?

**Laura Ellis** · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laura-ellis-profile/) · FY2016 to FY2025

For the 10 institutes with the most data science projects, I looked for the places where data science has room to grow: against each institute's own research areas and against disease burden. Each page shows one chart and the headline findings. The full reports are internal.

| Institute | Name | Data science share of funding |
|---|---|---:|

| [NHLBI](nhlbi.md) | National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute | 27% |

| [NIDA](nida.md) | National Institute on Drug Abuse | 29% |
| [NIDDK](niddk.md) | National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases | 19% |
| [NIGMS](nigms.md) | National Institute of General Medical Sciences | 23% |
| [NIMH](nimh.md) | National Institute of Mental Health | 32% |
| [NINDS](ninds.md) | National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke | 19% |
